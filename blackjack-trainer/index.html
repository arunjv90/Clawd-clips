Key Intuitions

Dealer 2–6 = Bust Cards

The dealer must hit until 17. With a low upcard, they have a ~35–42% bust probability. You can "stand pat" on stiff hands (12–16) and let the dealer bust.

Dealer 7–A = Strong Cards

A 7-upcard likely gives the dealer 17. You need to hit aggressively to compete, even risking a bust on your own stiff hands.

Always Double 11

With 11 you can't bust on one card, and any 10-value card (30% of deck) gives you 21. Doubling doubles your expected value.

Always Split A–A and 8–8

Two aces as one hand caps you at soft 12 — terrible. Split and each ace becomes a hand with a shot at 21. Two 8s = 16, the worst possible total. Split to escape.

Never Split 5–5 or 10–10

5+5 = 10, an excellent double opportunity. Don't split a great hand into two mediocre ones. 10+10 = 20 — you're almost certainly winning already.

Soft Hands: Free Hit

An ace can revert from 11 to 1. Soft 17 (A+6) won't bust on any single card. Always try to improve it; standing on soft 17 is a mistake.

Insurance is a Trap

Insurance pays 2:1 but the true odds of a dealer blackjack when showing an ace are ~31%. The bet loses money on average every time.

10-Value Cards = 30%+

There are more 10-value cards (10, J, Q, K) than any other rank. Always assume the dealer's hole card is a 10 — it's the most likely single value.
